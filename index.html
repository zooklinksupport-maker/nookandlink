1. Who is responsible

Nook & Link is an Android puzzle game provided by Anis Marzouk (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the app and this privacy-policy page. The publisher is responsible for the personal information it handles as described here. Google and the other providers named below also process information under their respective policies.

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact details below.

2. Information saved for gameplay

The app saves the following in its local storage on your device:

  • Completed levels, best stars, local weekly scores and progression.
  • Your unfinished route, saved completed routes and revealed hint.
  • Lives, refill timing, hint balance and tutorial state.
  • Your chosen nickname, built-in avatar, cover and audio/vibration preferences.

We use this information to run the game, resume your play and remember your choices. This version has no developer-operated game account, cloud save or online leaderboard. We do not upload your nickname or drawn routes to a developer-operated server or deliberately include them in ad requests.

Android backup and device-transfer services may copy app data according to your device settings and service provider. Local storage does not mean that operating-system backups are disabled.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise GPS location.

3. Google AdMob advertising

Nook & Link uses Google AdMob to show two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads you choose to watch, in exchange for an in-game reward such as a hint or a life. Declining does not require a purchase or prevent ordinary play, although the reward will not be granted.
  • Full-screen ads between levels, which are not optional and are not tied to a reward. These are paced: they begin only after the early levels, require several completed levels in between, and are spaced apart in time. You can close one and carry on playing.

Ad availability can vary, and an ad is only shown when one has loaded.

When advertising services run, Google's Mobile Ads SDK can automatically collect and share:

  • Your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, including Android advertising ID and app set ID, where available.
  • App/ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostics about app and advertising performance.

Google uses advertising data to provide and measure ads, support analytics, and prevent fraud. Personalization depends on applicable settings, eligibility and privacy choices. Information may be processed when services initialize or ads are requested/preloaded, before you choose to watch an ad. Choosing not to watch is not an advertising-data opt-out.

The app also uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain and store applicable privacy choices. We do not currently include a separate Firebase Analytics service.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure. Providers identified in the consent message may also process advertising information as described there.

4. Consent and your choices

Where required, the app presents a privacy message before requesting ads. That message explains the available purposes, providers and choices. Consent-based advertising processing relies on the consent you provide; this policy itself does not ask you to agree to personalized advertising.

To review or withdraw your advertising privacy choices, open the settings icon on the game's home screen, then tap “Privacy choices”. This reopens the same consent message, where you can change or withdraw a choice. Because that message is only presented where it is required, the “Privacy choices” option appears only when such a choice applies to you; where no consent message applies, the option is not shown. Changing or withdrawing a choice does not necessarily remove all ads. Ad availability and personalization depend on your choices and the applicable advertising configuration. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that already took place or by itself delete information Google already holds — see your rights and Google's own privacy tools for that.

You can also use Android's advertising controls to reset or delete your advertising ID, where supported, and visit Google My Ad Center. Device or Google-account controls do not necessarily replace choices made in the app's consent message. Non-personalized ads may still involve data processing for delivery, measurement or fraud prevention.

Core puzzles can be played offline. Advertising and rewarded offers require an internet connection.

5. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer you, address problems and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending passwords or unrelated sensitive information. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver this service.

Where European data-protection law applies, providing the requested gameplay functions and settings is based on delivering that service; handling support requests is based on our legitimate interest in assisting players, subject to your rights. We may retain information when necessary to meet legal obligations. Google's own processing grounds are explained in its policies and the applicable consent message.

6. Storage, deletion and security

Saved gameplay information remains on your device until it is replaced through play or removed. To delete it, use Android Settings → Apps → Nook & Link → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by device). This resets your progress, rewards and settings. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, but backups may remain or restore it later; manage those separately in your device or backup settings.

We cannot remotely retrieve or delete a saved game that exists only on your device. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations. Google's retention periods and deletion controls are described in its privacy policy; clearing this app's storage does not delete information already held by Google.

The game uses Android's app-private storage. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts transmitted user data using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

7. Providers and international processing

Information may be handled by Google for advertising and consent services, your operating-system backup provider if enabled, our email provider when you contact us, and GitHub when you visit this page. An advertisement may open another app or website, whose privacy policy will then apply.

These providers may process information outside your country. Google's policy describes its international processing and applicable transfer safeguards. The publisher may disclose information it holds when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to its processing, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not change the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Contact us about information the publisher holds. For information controlled by Google, Google's privacy tools and contact channels may also be needed. You can complain to your local data-protection authority. See the European Commission's explanation of individual data-protection rights.

9. Children and intended audience

Nook & Link is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern about a child's use of the game, please contact us.
